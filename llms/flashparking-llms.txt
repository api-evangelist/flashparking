# Flash (FlashParking)

> Flash is an Austin, Texas parking technology company selling an end-to-end mobility
> platform to parking asset owners and operators: FlashOS cloud software, PARCS gate
> and kiosk hardware, Flash Vision LPR cameras, valet and enforcement applications, EV
> charging, accounts-receivable automation, and a consumer Demand Network reached
> through its ParkWhiz/Arrive brands. Flash names nine partner APIs for Flash PARCS and
> Flash Valet, but publishes no base URL, no authentication model and no
> machine-readable specification for any of them; sandbox certification and production
> access are sold through sales@flashparking.com.

## Status of the API surface

- Machine-readable contract: NONE published. No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL,
  AsyncAPI, MCP server or A2A agent card was found on any Flash-controlled host as of
  2026-08-16.
- Access: commercially gated. Sandbox certification then paid production access,
  arranged with sales@flashparking.com.
- What IS published: a capability-level catalog of the nine APIs, dated product
  release notes, an Atlassian Statuspage, and named compliance certifications.

## APIs (documented by capability only)

- [eParking API](https://help.flashos.com/support/solutions/articles/60001639636-api-integrations-with-flash-parcs-and-flash-valet): inject, create, update and manage parking reservations in Flash PARCS and Flash Valet. Used by Ticketmaster, Tickets.com, SpotHero, LAZ Parking.
- [Locations API](https://help.flashos.com/support/solutions/articles/60001639636-api-integrations-with-flash-parcs-and-flash-valet): real-time facility occupancy, garage status (open/full/closed) and current active pricing, for signage, guidance systems and apps. Used by Daktronics, InfoTraffic, A LOT Media, Kode Labs, City of Las Vegas.
- [Monthly API](https://help.flashos.com/support/solutions/articles/60001639636-api-integrations-with-flash-parcs-and-flash-valet): create, update, edit and manage monthly parkers across their full lifecycle. Used by Paris, Zephire, Genea, AIMS, OpenPath.
- [Validations API](https://help.flashos.com/support/solutions/articles/60001639636-api-integrations-with-flash-parcs-and-flash-valet): search validations and redeem discounts in real time. Used by Westfield Century, Gator, TF Green Airport, Switch EV, Thanks Again.
- [Customer Service API](https://help.flashos.com/support/solutions/articles/60001639636-api-integrations-with-flash-parcs-and-flash-valet): call-center integration - ticket search (including by External ID as of Flash PARCS 4.0.12.0), vend gates, open and close support calls, video call a lane, process refunds, leave notes. Used by HelpMeParker, Umojo, ProPark.
- [Price Manager API](https://help.flashos.com/support/solutions/articles/60001639636-api-integrations-with-flash-parcs-and-flash-valet): check current rates, make real-time rate adjustments, schedule future rate changes. Used by SpotHero, infoTraffic.
- [FlashPass API](https://help.flashos.com/support/solutions/articles/60001639636-api-integrations-with-flash-parcs-and-flash-valet): purchase and manage FlashPasses for recurring visitors.
- [Vehicle Request API](https://help.flashos.com/support/solutions/articles/60001639636-api-integrations-with-flash-parcs-and-flash-valet): submit vehicle retrieval requests to valet staff.
- [Flash Receipt API](https://help.flashos.com/support/solutions/articles/60001639636-api-integrations-with-flash-parcs-and-flash-valet): pull receipts for parking transactions.
